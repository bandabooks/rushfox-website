About Us

A South African security company built on people and technology

100% black-owned, PSiRA-registered and headquartered in Mokopane, Limpopo.

Who we are

Rush Fox Security and CCTV (Pty) Ltd, trading as Rush Fox Security, is a 100% black-owned private security company registered with CIPC in May 2021 and with the Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority (PSiRA).

Headquartered in Mokopane, Limpopo, we protect retail stores, wholesalers, commercial properties and businesses across Mokopane and Polokwane. Our model combines disciplined, PSiRA-registered security officers on the ground with modern electronic security: CCTV surveillance, access control, alarms and perimeter protection, so every site is guarded, watched and recorded.

The company was founded by Malesela Fanny Banda in response to rising crime and the gap between what clients need and what the market was delivering. Our goal is to be the security partner that private and public sector clients can rely on: fully compliant, responsive and accountable.
