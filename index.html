About Rush Fox

Good security starts with understanding what you need to protect.

Protection with purpose.

Every site, team and community faces a different mix of risks. Rush Fox is built around a straightforward idea: start by listening, understand the environment, then bring the right people and tools together in a practical plan.

Our story is one of partnership—making security easier to understand, coordinate and improve. We aim to be a dependable partner, responsive to the realities on the ground and focused on the outcomes that matter to each client.

01 / OUR VISION

A safer, more confident future

To help create environments where people can work, live and thrive with greater peace of mind.

02 / OUR MISSION

Make protection practical

To provide considered security services and solutions shaped around each client’s needs.

03 / OUR VALUES

Trust, care, accountability

We value integrity, respect, vigilance and clear communication in every relationship.

Why Rush Fox? A joined-up view of people, physical security, technology and risk—so decisions can be informed by the whole picture, not just one part of it.
