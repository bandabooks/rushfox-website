What we do

Guarding and technology from one accountable provider

Officers protect, technology detects and our team responds. Every site gets disciplined, PSiRA-registered security officers and the electronic systems to watch, record and control access.

Static & Site Guarding

Day and night shift security officers (Grades A to C; D and E as required) for retail, commercial, industrial, residential and government sites.

Manned Access Control

Gatehouse and entrance control: visitor and vehicle registers, searches, permits and key control.

CCTV Surveillance Systems

Design, supply and installation of HD analogue and IP camera systems: bullet, dome, PTZ, night-vision and 4MP/8MP (4K) cameras with NVR/DVR recording.

Electronic Access Control

Biometric (fingerprint and face), card and tag readers, keypads, time-and-attendance and door controllers, designed to keep emergency exits compliant.

Intruder Alarm Systems

Wired and wireless alarms, PIR and beam detectors, panic buttons and armed-response linking.

Electric Fencing & Perimeter Detection

Electric fence installation and repair by a registered electric fence installer, with certificate of compliance; outdoor beams and perimeter detection.
