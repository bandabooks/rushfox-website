Power outages create the exact conditions criminals look for: dark premises, gates that cannot close and alarms that may not report. Your security plan should assume the power will go off.

## List what must keep running

Start with the essentials: the **CCTV recorder, cameras and network switch**, the **alarm panel and its communicator**, the **electric fence energiser** and **gate or boom motors**. Note how long each must run without mains power.

## Choose the right backup

- **UPS units** keep recorders, switches and small loads running and protect them from surges when the power returns.
- **Battery backup** built into alarm panels, energisers and gate motors must be tested and replaced on schedule.
- **Inverter and solar systems** suit sites that need longer runtimes or must keep several systems running at once.

## Install it safely

Electrical work on inverters and solar systems must be done and certified (certificate of compliance) by a registered electrician. Do not overload a UPS with equipment it was not sized for.

## Test it regularly

Switch off the mains supply on purpose, at a safe time, and check that cameras keep recording, the alarm reports, the fence stays live and gates can still be operated. Batteries wear out, so record when each was last replaced.
