Services

Complete security: people, technology and expertise

From a single guard post to multi-site surveillance, access control and perimeter protection, with full after-sales support.

Service line 1

Manned Guarding & Protection

Our PSiRA-registered security officers are the visible deterrent on every site. They are vetted, uniformed, equipped and supervised, and work to written post instructions agreed with the client.

Static & Site Guarding

Day and night shift security officers (Grades A to C; D and E as required) for retail, commercial, industrial, residential and government sites.

Manned Access Control

Gatehouse and entrance control: visitor and vehicle registers, searches, permits and key control.

Patrols & Supervision

Foot and vehicle patrols, site inspections and mobile supervisors to keep standards high.

Retail Security & Loss Prevention

Uniformed and plain-clothes officers, exit checks and shrinkage control for stores and wholesalers.

Event Security & Crowd Control

Planning and staffing for events, functions, meetings and public gatherings.

Close Protection (VIP)

Bodyguarding and protection of executives and dignitaries, by arrangement.

Reaction & Response

Response to alarm activations and incidents at contracted sites, by arrangement.

Car Watch & Parking Security

Parking area protection for shopping centres, offices and events.

Construction & Project Site Security

Asset protection for construction sites, plant, materials and stock yards.

Service line 2

Electronic Security & Technology

We design, install, commission and maintain integrated electronic security systems, from a single camera to multi-site surveillance, access control and perimeter protection, with full after-sales support.

CCTV Surveillance Systems

Design, supply and installation of HD analogue and IP camera systems: bullet, dome, PTZ, night-vision and 4MP/8MP (4K) cameras with NVR/DVR recording.

AI Video Analytics & LPR

Line-crossing, intrusion and person/vehicle detection; licence plate recognition (LPR/ANPR) for gates and car parks.

Remote Viewing & Monitoring

Client live view and playback on mobile and desktop, event alerts and cloud backup; off-site monitoring arranged per site.

Electronic Access Control

Biometric (fingerprint and face), card and tag readers, keypads, time-and-attendance and door controllers, designed to keep emergency exits compliant.

Turnstiles, Boom Gates & Automation

Pedestrian turnstiles, boom gates, sliding and swing gate motors and garage automation.

Intercom & Video Intercom

Audio and video intercom systems for gates, complexes and offices.

Intruder Alarm Systems

Wired and wireless alarms, PIR and beam detectors, panic buttons and armed-response linking.

Electric Fencing & Perimeter Detection

Electric fence installation and repair by a registered electric fence installer, with certificate of compliance; outdoor beams and perimeter detection.

Fire & Smoke Detection

Smoke and heat detection integrated with alarm systems; certified fire installations scoped with accredited fire-detection partners.

Networking & Connectivity

Structured cabling, PoE switching, Wi-Fi bridges and 4G/LTE links for remote sites.

Backup Power for Security

UPS, inverter and solar backup for cameras, alarms and gates; electrical work certified (CoC) by a registered electrician.

Locksmith & Physical Security

Locks, safes, burglar bars and security doors to harden premises.

Service line 3

Security Consulting & Risk Management

Security Risk Assessments & Audits

On-site surveys and written risk reports with practical recommendations.

CCTV Layout Planning

Camera placement plans and coverage analysis before any installation.

Officer Training & Site Induction

Site-specific induction, post instructions and refresher training for officers.

Security Plans & Procedures

Post orders, emergency procedures and access control policies for your site.

Maintenance & Service Level Agreements

Preventive maintenance, health checks, firmware updates, repairs and fault call-outs for systems we install and for existing systems we take over.

How we work

Our five-step approach

1

Site Risk Assessment

We inspect the site, identify threats and weak points, and review existing measures.

2

Security Plan & Design

A written proposal: officer deployment, camera layout, access points and costs.

3

Deploy & Install

Officers are placed, briefed and equipped; systems are installed and commissioned.

4

Monitor & Supervise

Supervisor visits, CCTV review, occurrence records and incident handling.

5

Report & Improve

Regular client reporting, reviews and recommendations as risks change.
